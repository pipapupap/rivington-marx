RIVINGTON MARX — STUDY 08 / THE EYE-LINE HANG

Desktop design study, designed at 1600 × 1000. No mobile version.
Open study-8/index.html (or the hub index.html, button 08).

FILES
index.html, styles.css, script.js   the study
vendor/                             GSAP 3.12.5 + Observer, stored locally
Images: ../assets/*.jpg (shared with Study 07 and Studies 1-6), ../assets/thumbs/ (480px
copies for frames, filmstrip and contact sheet), ../assets/logo-ivory.png
and ../assets/monogram-ivory.png.

HOW IT BEHAVES
Home       Seven portrait frames in one row on a dark wall. No scroll.
           RM monogram intro plays once per browser session.
           Hover/focus a frame: it grows slightly, the others dim, and a gallery
           label appears beneath.
           The wall takes an opaque shade of the hovered interior.
           ← → move along the row, Enter opens, Esc returns.
Project    The frame grows into the centre stage. Images travel vertically
           (wheel, ↑ ↓, click the image, or the filmstrip on the left).
           The wall re-tones with each image. Details + raises a drawer.
           ← Previous project · Next project → along the bottom.
Work       Contact sheet of every photograph. Hover lights one project.
           Click grows the photo into the same project window.
           Index / Sheet (bottom right) swaps to a list with a preview column.
Services   Six full-screen chapters, one word each, vertical snap scrolling.
           Click a chapter: an ivory sheet slides in with copy, two photos that
           travel vertically, and Begin a project.
Studio     One frame alone on the wall; Details + reveals text and press.
Contact    Drawer from the right with details and a four-field form
           (the form does not send anything in this study).

NOTE ON WORDS
The header shows Work · Services · Studio · Contact (four words, one more than
the research budget) so Services is reachable from every screen.
