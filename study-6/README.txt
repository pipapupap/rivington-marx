RIVINGTON MARX — STUDY 06
The Daylight Atelier / Research Study B

Desktop HTML design concept. Best viewed at 1280px or wider.
Open index.html directly, or serve outputs/ and visit /study-6/.
The hub includes a Study 6 button that opens in a new tab.

DIRECTION
Chalk-white surfaces, pale sage, soft sand, olive-charcoal type, and a muted
clay accent. Large client photographs form asymmetrical visual chapters.
Bodoni Moda headings and DM Sans captions/navigation are served by Google
Fonts; system serif and sans-serif fallbacks work without font access.

BASED ON THE RESEARCH FOR STUDY B
Amari: differences in photographic scale, immersive imagery, short copy.
Anna: an immediate photographic opening and clear project access.
Harlowe: pale surfaces, spacious composition, disciplined typography.
Earlier references: The UQ's arrow gallery and Olson Kundig's clear separation
of portfolio, practice, and people. This is an original composition, not a
reproduction of a reference template.

INTERACTIONS
- Layered page chapters: a chapter's closing viewport holds while the next
  chapter scrolls over it, inspired by the Anna reference. Tall chapters remain
  readable in full. Reduced motion removes the sticky behavior and reveals.
- The blue salon's right image column aligns at the top of the main photograph
  and scrolls vertically through three client images and their captions.
- One contained horizontal detail gallery: arrows, keyboard arrows, native
  horizontal gestures, or vertical wheel input anywhere in the gallery section.
  Wheel scrolling returns to the page at the gallery's beginning and end.
- Photographs and visible story links open full-window editorial collections.
  Scroll through the photographs; switch stories with previous/next controls;
  close with Escape or the close button. The inquiry link returns to Contact.
- Studio tabs change the photograph and accompanying approach text.
- An expanded photographic navigation panel, anchor navigation, and real
  email/phone links. No autoplay or timed image cycling.
- Keyboard focus, native modal focus containment, descriptive image text,
  reduced-motion support, and a desktop-only notice below 980px.

ASSETS AND DEPLOYMENT
All photographs come from the client's existing shared ../assets/ folder.
No new photographs, per-study asset copies, framework, build step, analytics,
or form backend. Keep study-6/ beside assets/ inside the deployment root.
Upload the updated hub and study-6/ to the existing repository to publish.
The original Studies 1–5 are unchanged. This work has been saved locally only.

REVISION NOTES
The opening point-of-view section has been removed, with its navigation links
cleaned up. The light-chapter headline is a single line without the smaller
paragraph. The plus-and-statement block, colour chapter headline, and studio
headline have been removed. The horizontal detail gallery still supports
arrows and wheel input across its entire section, releasing at either end.
The light chapter's right images now align at the top and share square frames;
the colour chapter's three right-column frames are square too. Portfolio wheel
input accepts diagonal trackpad gestures and no longer snaps small movements
back to the current image. CSS and JS URLs are versioned to refresh cached files.

Collection names and descriptive copy are editorial concept labels, not
assertions that photographs belong to specific built projects. Project
groupings and final copy should be confirmed before a production launch.
